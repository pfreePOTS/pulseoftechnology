About PulseOne

Our Team is Your Team

PulseOne is a strategic technology advisory and IT integration firm. Since 2002, we've helped small and mid-sized organizations navigate the technology decisions that matter.

We are passionate about the power of people and technology to transform a company. We are confident we can significantly enhance your business objectives and profitability.

With team members across the United States and internationally, we combine the responsiveness of a local partner with the depth of an enterprise-level back office.

Our History

Two Decades of Doing It Right

2002
Founded in Ventura, CA
We started as a regional IT services company focused on strategic and day-to-day technology needs for small and mid-sized businesses.
2016
Acquired ICS
We acquired a business services company, ICS, and added nation-wide project management and service management capabilities to our existing service offerings.
2020
National Expansion
We launched a growth initiative to open offices in local communities close to our existing team members, adding locations in Montana, New Jersey, Tennessee and expanded locations in Southern California.
Now & Future
National Reach, Local Heart
As a global IT services company, we prioritize supporting local businesses and business owners with the same dedication to customer service and relationships that have been our cornerstone.
